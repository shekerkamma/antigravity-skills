# Sales Intelligence & Win/Loss Analyzer

Use this skill to mine sales recordings (Zoom, Fireflies, Gong, tl;dv) and CRM notes for qualitative patterns that drive revenue.

## Comparative Win/Loss Diagnostic

Ingest call transcripts and compare **CLOSED-WON** against **CLOSED-LOST** conversations across:

1. **Core Problem Language:** Exact words and pain points articulated by prospects.
2. **Urgency Triggers:** What timeline drivers existed in won deals vs lost deals.
3. **Objection Profiling:**
   - *Fatal Objections:* Objections that consistently killed deals.
   - *False Alarm Objections:* Objections raised frequently that did NOT prevent purchases.
4. **Pricing & Budget Reactions:** Prospect reactions to pricing tiers, discount requests, ROI expectations.
5. **Competitive Mentions:** Competitors named, perceived strengths/weaknesses.
6. **Stakeholder Dynamics:** Multi-threading and decision-maker involvement patterns.

## Output Action Plan
- **Winning Playbook:** Common themes, hooks, and discovery questions in won deals.
- **Sales Rep Coaching:** What reps should start doing, stop doing, and handle differently.
- **Marketing & Positioning Signals:** Messaging revisions and positioning adjustments to pre-empt fatal objections before sales calls.
