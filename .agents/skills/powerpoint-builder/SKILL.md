# PowerPoint Builder — Executive 10-Slide Deck Generator

Use this skill to convert business research, competitive intelligence, or strategic plans into structured, presentation-ready slide decks.

## Narrative Structure (The 10-Slide Framework)

Every generated presentation follows this battle-tested executive structure:

1. **Slide 1: Executive Summary** — Core thesis, BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front), and ultimate conclusion.
2. **Slide 2: Current Situation** — Context, baseline state, and market/internal triggers.
3. **Slide 3: Key Findings** — Top 3 empirical takeaways from research and data.
4. **Slide 4: Important Numbers** — Critical metrics, financial impact, and unit economics.
5. **Slide 5: Main Opportunity** — High-leverage upside and strategic wedge.
6. **Slide 6: Biggest Risks & Vulnerabilities** — Unvarnished risk landscape and mitigation paths.
7. **Slide 7: Available Options** — Trade-off comparison between 2–4 distinct strategic choices.
8. **Slide 8: Recommended Option** — Specific, evidence-backed recommendation with clear rationale.
9. **Slide 9: Implementation Plan** — Phased execution roadmap (30/60/90 days or Sprint milestones).
10. **Slide 10: Next Steps & Immediate Decisions** — Who owns what, by when, and key success metrics.

## Executive Presentation Rules
- **One Major Idea per Slide:** Clear message per slide.
- **Action Titles (Accenture/McKinsey Style):** Slide titles are substantive assertions, not passive labels.
- **Facts vs. Assumptions:** Explicitly separate verified facts from assumptions.
- **Zero Fluff:** Strict bullet point discipline; no walls of generic text.
