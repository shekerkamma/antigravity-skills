# Executive Report & Decision Memo Writer

Use this skill to write authoritative, comprehensive executive reports that founders, board members, or clients can read in 5 minutes to make informed decisions.

## Document Template & Specification

### 1. Executive Summary
- Concise synthesis of what was investigated, why it matters now, and the ultimate strategic conclusion (BLUF).

### 2. Key Numbers & Metrics
- Curated table of the vital metrics impacting the decision (TAM, CAC, margins, velocity, timelines).

### 3. Main Findings & Patterns
- Deep-dive into empirical patterns, market signals, customer feedback, and internal benchmarks.

### 4. Business Impact
- Direct financial and operational implications for the organization.

### 5. Recommended Actions
For every single recommendation, enforce the 5-point standard:
- **What:** Specific tactical action.
- **Why:** Underlying business driver.
- **Evidence:** Concrete data point or quote from the notebook.
- **Expected Impact:** Quantified upside or efficiency gain.
- **Key Risk & Mitigation:** Potential pitfall and safety hedge.

### 6. Limitations & Unresolved Questions
- Transparent accounting of data voids, assumptions, and confidence boundaries.

### 7. Immediate Next Steps
- Concrete 14-day action list with owner and deliverable.
