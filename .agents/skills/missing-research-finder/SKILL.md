# Missing Research Finder

Use this skill to spot blind spots in business research before committing budget, hiring, or launching new products.

## Gap Classification Protocol

Based on all materials provided, categorize every research void into three tiers:

### 1. Critical (Decision-Blockers)
*Information we MUST know before making the decision.*
- Lack of data on willingness to pay, unit economics, regulatory limits, or core operational bottlenecks.

### 2. Important (Confidence-Builders)
*Information that would significantly increase our confidence.*
- Secondary competitor pricing tiers, segment-specific churn rates, conversion benchmarks.

### 3. Nice to Have (Contextual)
*Useful context but unlikely to change the go/no-go decision.*
- Long-tail market trends, tangential feature wishlists.

---

## Per-Gap Analysis Specification

For every identified gap, supply:
- **What is missing:** Exact metric, data point, or qualitative insight.
- **Why it matters:** Specific business risk or opportunity tied to this gap.
- **The question to answer:** The precise question that research must resolve.
- **Recommended source type:** Strongest evidence channel (e.g., customer discovery calls, SEC filings, primary survey, pricing scrape, Reddit/forum threads, API benchmarks).

*Rule: Prioritize information that could change the decision. Avoid recommending research simply for the sake of collecting more links.*
